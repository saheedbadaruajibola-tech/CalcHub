About CalcHub

Free tools that just work.

Our mission

CalcHub provides fast, accurate, and free online calculators for everyday decisions — from mortgages and loans to BMI and date math. No signup, no downloads, no nonsense.

Why we built this

Most calculator sites are cluttered with popups, slow-loading ads, and confusing interfaces. We built CalcHub to be the opposite: clean, instant, and mobile-friendly.

How it works

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is ever sent to a server. Your data stays on your device.

Our calculators

  • Finance: Mortgage, Loan, Compound Interest, Tip, Discount, Salary
  • Health: BMI, Calorie, Pregnancy Due Date
  • Daily Life: Age, Percentage, Date Difference, GPA
  • Utility: Currency Converter
